About us

36 years with Novalja

A family story that grows with the island.

photo_cameraportrait 4:5The Novalis department store in the centre of Novalja: the facade and the street in morning light, with passers-by. The owners' family in front of the entrance would also work.

Novalis began as a small family business in the centre of Novalja, with a shop at the heart of the business. As the needs of the people who live, work and holiday here changed, our story grew with them.

We widened our range, opened new premises and took up new lines of business. Step by step, a small shop grew into a company that today brings together retail, building and home improvement, hospitality, accommodation and wholesale. Our family character and our ties to the island of Pag are still the foundation of what we do.

Part of island life

We grew by listening to what the island needs

Everyday shopping, doing up your home, getting ready for the tourist season or running a business - island life calls for a range close at hand and a partner you can rely on.

photo_cameralandscape 3:2Inside Hiper Novalis or Hiper Bau: a wide shot of the shop floor with customers and staff at work.
photo_cameraportrait 3:4Valis or Pod Zvon: a detail of service, e.g. coffee on the terrace, cakes in the display case or a dish from the open hearth.

Hiper Novalis and Market Novalis cover everyday shopping, while Hiper Bau offers everything for building and home improvement. Valis brings together a bistro, a pastry shop and a coffee house, and Pod Zvon keeps up the traditional Dalmatian way of cooking. Bed & Breakfast Novalis offers guests a pleasant stay in the town centre, and through our wholesale and delivery services we support business customers.

What connects us

Our different businesses share one approach: understand what people need and build a range that makes living and doing business on the island easier.

Our mission

More possibilities for life on the island.

Our mission is to make everyday life on the island easier for residents, guests and business partners, with a range close at hand, good service and relationships built on trust.

photo_cameralandscape 4:3Life on the island: Novalja from the sea or from the air, or a Novalis delivery on an island road.

We want you to be able to meet as many of your needs as possible right here - from shopping and fitting out your space to hospitality, accommodation and supplies for your business.

That is why we keep investing in our range and our service, listening to our customers and giving each partner a personal approach. The company's family character and its ties to Novalja remain the foundation of how we work.
