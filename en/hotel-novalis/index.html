B&B Novalis: Your oasis in the city centre.

Decorated with care and love, in the very centre of Novalja. Relax in comfortable rooms with king-size beds or in a hydromassage tub on the roof terrace, with a view of the sea or of Novalja's rooftops and bell towers.

Josipa Kunkere 8, Novalja (new tab)

Call: 053 662 222

What we offer

On the roof there are two terraces with hydromassage tubs, a buffet breakfast and a bar, and below them rooms to relax in, in the very centre of town. We also offer our guests free parking and tips for trips around the island, to places both well known and still undiscovered.

  • Hydromassage tubs and sunbeds
  • Buffet breakfast
  • Bar with a wide choice of drinks
  • Air conditioning and fast Wi-Fi
  • LCD TV, minibar and safe
  • Bathroom with a walk-in shower
