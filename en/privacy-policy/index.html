Privacy policy

What personal data we collect, why, how long we keep it and how you can exercise your rights.

  • We use the data from your enquiry only to reply to you.
  • We use job applications and CVs only to consider the application; with your consent we keep them for up to 12 months.
  • We never sell or rent out your data.
  • For questions and requests, write to us at info@novalis.com.hr.

Novalis d.o.o. started out in a small space in the very centre of Novalja as a family business, mainly in retail, and over time has become a significant and reliable business partner on the island of Pag. In the course of our business we collect and process personal data provided to us by customers, clients and visitors to this website. Because protecting your privacy is a priority for us, we have adopted this Privacy Policy in line with the General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679, the GDPR) and the Croatian Act on the Implementation of the General Data Protection Regulation.

1. Data controller

The controller of your personal data and of this website is:

Novalis d.o.o.
Josipa Kunkere 8, 53291 Novalja, Croatia
Phone: +385 53 662 001
Email: info@novalis.com.hr

For any questions about the processing of your personal data and to exercise your rights, contact us by email or by post at the address above.

2. Our principles

Novalis d.o.o. follows these principles to protect your privacy:

  • we do not collect more data than we need,
  • we do not use your data for purposes not set out in this Policy,
  • we do not keep your data once it is no longer needed,
  • we never sell, lend or publish your personal data,
  • data is received only by the service providers we use to run the website and our business, and only to the extent their service requires (section 10),
  • we do not use automated decision-making or profiling,
  • we make sure your data is stored securely.

3. Enquiries by form, email or phone

When you send us an enquiry using the form on this website, by email or by phone, we process the data you give us so that we can reply to you.

Data
your name, email address and the content of your message; if you provide them, also your phone number, company name or other details in your message.
Purpose
replying to your enquiry and, if it leads to working together, preparing an offer or a contract.
Legal basis
taking steps at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in replying to the enquiries we receive (Article 6(1)(f)).
Retention
for as long as we are in contact about your enquiry. If the enquiry leads to a business relationship, for the duration of the contract and afterwards for as long as the law requires (for example accounting and tax rules).

Giving us your data is voluntary, but without your name and email address we cannot reply to you.

4. Job applications

When you send us an application and CV using the form on the Careers page, we process the data you give us in order to consider your application.

Data
your name, email address, CV (a PDF or Word file) with all the details you include in it and, if you fill them in, your phone number, location or position and the text of your message. Please do not send us information we do not ask for.
Purpose
considering your application and, where needed, contacting you for an interview or to offer you employment, and considering you for future vacancies.
Legal basis
taking steps at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Keeping your application to consider you for future vacancies is based on your consent (Article 6(1)(a)), which you give by ticking a separate, optional box in the form and can withdraw at any time.
Retention
until a decision on the vacancy is made. If you have given your consent, we keep your application for future vacancies and delete it no later than 12 months after we receive it.
Recipients
your application is received by our HR department by email at kadrovska@novalis.com.hr. The message is sent by the email service provider Brevo SAS, and the form is received by the website's hosting provider Vercel Inc. (section 10). The file is not published or stored on this website.

Giving us your data is voluntary, but without your name, contact details and CV we cannot consider your application. The email service provider may keep a copy of the message in its logs for up to 30 days, after which it is deleted. You can withdraw your consent and ask for your application to be deleted by writing to kadrovska@novalis.com.hr.

5. News about our offers

We send you news about new products and services only if you have given your explicit consent.

Data
your name and email address.
Purpose
sending you news about what we offer.
Legal basis
your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR).
Retention
until you withdraw your consent.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to info@novalis.com.hr. After that we will no longer send you news. Withdrawing your consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal.

6. Visiting the website

When you open this website, your browser automatically sends the server the technical data it sends to every website. Without it the website cannot be displayed.

Data
IP address, date and time of access, the address of the page opened, the page you came from, and your browser and device type.
Purpose
displaying the website, keeping it secure and working properly, and protecting it against misuse and attacks.
Legal basis
our legitimate interest in a secure and properly working website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
Retention
the logs are kept by the website's hosting provider (Vercel, section 10) and deleted automatically after a limited period. We do not link them with other data and do not use them to find out who you are.

7. Map of locations

We show the maps on the home page and on the location pages using the free OpenFreeMap service (data © OpenStreetMap contributors), provided by Hyperknot Software Kft., Hungary. The map loads only when you scroll to it. When it loads, your browser downloads parts of the map from that service's servers, so the service sees your IP address and which part of the map you are viewing. The map is delivered through the Cloudflare network (Cloudflare, Inc., USA), which also sees your IP address (section 10). According to its own policy, OpenFreeMap uses no cookies, does not store IP addresses in its access logs and deletes its logs after 7 days. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in showing you where our locations are (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Show my location

On the map on the home page you can press the “Show my location” button. Only then does your browser ask for permission to access your location. We use your location only in your browser, to show it on the map; we do not send it to our servers or store it there. When the map moves to your location, your browser downloads the parts of the map for that area from the OpenFreeMap servers. The legal basis is the consent you give in your browser (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw the permission at any time in your browser settings.

8. Cookies

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser. This website uses no cookies or similar technologies (such as local storage in the browser), uses no analytics tools such as Google Analytics, and does not track your movements on the site. That is why we do not ask for your consent to cookies.

If this changes, we will ask for your consent before setting any cookies that are not essential for the website to work, and update this Policy.

9. Links to other websites

The addresses of our locations and the Directions buttons open Google Maps, and some links lead to other websites (for example hotelnovalis.hr). When you open such a link, you leave this website, and the owner of the other website is responsible for processing data there, under its own privacy policy.

10. Recipients and transfers outside the EU

Your data is received only by the service providers we use to run the website and our business, and only to the extent their service requires:

  • Vercel Inc., USA: website hosting, delivery of its content and receipt of the job application form,
  • Hyperknot Software Kft. (OpenFreeMap), Hungary: map of locations,
  • Cloudflare, Inc., USA: map delivery for the OpenFreeMap service,
  • Brevo SAS, France: sending the message with your application to our HR department,
  • our email provider: receiving and storing messages.

The website's fonts, icons and programs, including the one that displays the map, load directly from this website. Apart from the map (section 7), the website loads nothing from other companies' servers.

We may also give data to the competent authorities where the law requires us to.

Vercel Inc. and Cloudflare, Inc. are based in the USA. Transfers of data to the USA are based on the European Commission's adequacy decision of 10 July 2023 for the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, in which both companies participate.

The message with your application is sent, and kept in its logs, by Brevo SAS, based in France; according to its own terms, Brevo keeps its infrastructure in France and Belgium and uses standard contractual clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework for transfers to the USA (for example the Cloudflare network).

11. Data security

We have put security measures in place to protect the personal data you share with us. Your personal data is protected against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss or alteration, and against unauthorised disclosure of or access to personal data that is transmitted, stored or otherwise processed. The website opens only over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

12. Minors

If you are under 16, you need the consent of a parent or another holder of parental responsibility before giving us your personal data.

13. Your rights

With regard to your personal data, you have the right:

  • of access: to find out whether we process your data and to get a copy of it (Article 15 GDPR),
  • to have inaccurate data corrected and incomplete data completed (Article 16),
  • to have your data erased when it is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected for or when it is processed unlawfully (Article 17),
  • to restriction of processing, for example while we check whether the data is accurate (Article 18),
  • to data portability: to receive the data you have given us in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format (Article 20),
  • to object to processing based on our legitimate interest, and at any time to processing for sending news about our offers (Article 21),
  • to withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal (Article 7(3)).

Send your request to info@novalis.com.hr or by post to Novalis d.o.o., Josipa Kunkere 8, 53291 Novalja, Croatia. We will reply without undue delay and within one month of receiving your request at the latest. Exercising your rights is free of charge. If we cannot reliably establish who sent the request, we will ask for additional proof of identity.

You can also send a request to delete your job application to kadrovska@novalis.com.hr.

14. Complaint to the supervisory authority

If you believe that the processing of your personal data does not comply with the law, you can lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority at any time:

Agencija za zaštitu osobnih podataka (AZOP)

15. Personal data breaches

If a personal data breach occurs that could put your rights at risk, we will report it to AZOP no later than 72 hours after becoming aware of it. If the breach is likely to result in a high risk to your rights and freedoms, we will also inform you without undue delay.

16. Changes to this policy

We may change this Policy when our services, this website or the law change. We will announce any changes on this page, and the date of the last change is shown at the top.
