Hiper Novalis: From groceries to boating gear.

Across 14,000 m² you will find a supermarket, drinks wholesale, a furniture showroom, an appliances department, mobile and landline phones and phone accessories, and much more. Shop in 7,000 m² of indoor space on two floors, with a large covered car park.

What we offer

Two floors and ten departments: groceries, delicatessen, a restaurant, drinks and everything for the beach and garden on the ground floor, and furniture, appliances, computers, school supplies and everything else for the home upstairs. Some of the products we offer are unique in Croatia, because we source and import ourselves everything that is in demand but cannot be found in this region.
